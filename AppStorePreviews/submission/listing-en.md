# Fireside apps — English App Store copy (draft)

This is a local draft for the English App Store localization. The official app names remain **炉边** and **炉边·随身**. Review every claim against the signed release builds before entering it in App Store Connect.

| What's New (1.0) | Meet three original companions, choose your avatar, and manage your conversations and memories. |

### Description

Some days, you just want a place to finish what you were saying.

炉边·随身 is a character-chat app for iPhone. Choose from three original companions with different personalities, talk about everyday moments, and return to conversations that continue over time.

**Find the right companion.** Switch between characters whenever you like. Each has a different way of responding, and each conversation is saved separately.

**Keep what matters.** Your companion can remember some preferences and shared moments from your chats. You can inspect, edit, or delete memories, or clear a character's conversation.

**Show up as yourself.** Choose an original avatar and set the name and introduction you want your companion to know.

**Choose your AI provider.** Claude, OpenAI, and DeepSeek are supported. Full AI conversations require your own valid API key and an internet connection. Without a key, only limited scripted replies are available. Chat content is sent to the provider you select to generate responses.

The app stores conversations and memories in your iCloud Documents when available, or on this device otherwise. 炉边·随身 is a virtual character-chat app, not a medical, mental-health treatment, or emergency service.

### Description

A conversation can feel different when your companion is right there with you.

Built for Apple Vision Pro, 炉边 brings original 3D characters into your space. Choose a companion, talk by text or voice, and see her respond with expressions and movement.

**A companion in your room.** Meet characters with distinct personalities and talk face to face in a familiar space.

**Character Workshop.** Customize a character's hair, outfit, color, and voice.

**Natural interaction.** On Apple Vision Pro, use hand gestures to greet or guide a character. Hold a fist to speak. Hand tracking and room-aware movement must be used on a physical device; the simulator does not provide full sensing.

**Movement that fits the moment.** Choose Stay, Follow, or Explore mode. You can also use the app without voice permissions by typing.

**Choose your AI provider.** Full AI conversations require your own API key for Claude, OpenAI, or DeepSeek and an internet connection. Without a key, only limited scripted replies are available. Chat content is sent to the selected provider. Apple system speech is available; optional Fish Audio cloud voices send text for speech generation to Fish Audio.

Conversations and memories use your iCloud Documents when available, or on-device storage otherwise. 炉边 is a virtual character-chat app, not a medical, mental-health treatment, or emergency service.
